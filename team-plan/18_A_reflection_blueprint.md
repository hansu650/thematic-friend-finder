## How to develop an answer

For an important incident, explain the situation and your responsibility, the choice you made and why, the evidence of its effect, the limits or alternative interpretation, and what you would change next time. Separate a recorded result from your judgement about it. Analyse a few meaningful incidents instead of listing every commit or screenshot.

Use “I” for your action and learning, and “we” for a supported team decision or outcome. Keep peers' contributions visible. AI-assisted implementation should be acknowledged consistently with the actual project record; explain what you understood, checked, changed and could reproduce yourself.
