Role: **Developer and Usability Lead**. Investigate two relevant products and propose a small improvement for the next iteration. The selected implementation is a Spring Boot website; an observed mobile app can still inform a web interaction.

## Research focus

Compare navigation between home, discovery, connections, chat and profiles; clarity of like/skip controls; mobile layouts, empty states and errors.

Propose three interface improvements, each with a short reason and evidence.

Use actual pages, official product screenshots or official help. Record the URL, access date and observation method. Distinguish documentation reading from a feature you personally tested. No purchase or contact with strangers is required.

## Deliverable

Submit approximately **500–800 English words** in Markdown: two products and official sources; at least two page/flow evidence references; two useful practices and one practice you would avoid with reasons; the role-specific output above; one candidate user story, at least two observable acceptance conditions and one discussion question.

Suggested story: “As a user, I want to find the main actions on my phone so that I can complete the friendship flow without confusion.” Write acceptance as a starting condition, action and observable outcome, not simply “works correctly”.
