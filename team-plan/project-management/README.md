A diagram version is page 10 of the [ten-page Draw.io pack](../../code/thematic-friend/docs/design/README.md). The [verification record](./VERIFICATION.md) describes formula, saved-file and visual checks, including the authoring runtime's exit limitation.

The [Scrum and evidence workbook](scrum-and-evidence.xlsx) is retained as an optional template. It contains the six teaching weeks, proposed effort, backlog, evidence register, meeting/actions, test/observation records and PDP fields. It is not the daily management entry point.

The older [six-person-gantt.xlsx](./six-person-gantt.xlsx), critical-path Draw.io sources and their verification record are retained as the 13 November planning baseline. They use the former 188-hour allocation and November delivery-week numbering. Their inputs and dates are not completion evidence; do not use them as the current workload or university deadline.

The current proposal is 208 h: A 50, C 40, D 34 and B/E/F 28 each. Actual capacity, actual hours, attendance, decisions, participant feedback and final results remain blank until supplied or observed. Amber fields in the workbook are editable.

The workbook does not update Trello or submit coursework. Prefer one authoritative record per result and link to it from the board; use permanent evidence links and real dates.
