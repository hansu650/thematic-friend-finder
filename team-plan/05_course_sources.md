# Course Sources and Use

The course PDFs and templates are available in the local coursework archive or Moodle and are not redistributed in this repository. Source titles and page references are retained below.

This plan applies Software Development Process methods to the Thematic Project. Example companies, people and submission rules in other labs are not facts about this project.

| Local source | Relevant material | Use here |
|---|---|---|
| Lec06 Project Management (course copy) | Tasks, dependencies, Gantt charts, critical paths and slack | Macro schedule and replanning |
| Lec07 Scrum (course copy), pp. 11–19 | Fixed Sprints, accountabilities, stories, backlogs and events | Two two-week Sprints after preparation |
| Lab07 Scrum (course copy), pp. 1–2 | Roles, estimates, capacity and a working increment | Members confirm availability; review integrated software |
| Lec08 Kanban and Lean (course copy), pp. 5–11, 14–18 | Boards, WIP, bottlenecks and improvement | Actual flow and initial WIP limits |
| Lab08 Kanban (course copy), pp. 1–2 | Workflow choices and cards | Five lists are a team choice |
| Assessment specification (course copy), pp. 4–9 | 25% demonstration, 75% reflection, 15-minute video maximum and personal evidence | Weekly evidence and final checks |
| Reflection template (course copy) | Project, teamwork, management, ethics/legal and professional development | Personal evidence and reflection structure |
| Tuckman slides (course copy), pp. 3–9 | Five team-development stages | Analyse real collaboration events |

Supporting official references: [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html), [Trello card members](https://support.atlassian.com/trello/docs/adding-a-member-to-a-card/), [Trello formatting](https://support.atlassian.com/trello/docs/how-to-format-your-text-in-trello/) and [checklists](https://support.atlassian.com/trello/docs/adding-checklists-to-cards/). The planning materials were checked on 6 November 2026.

Dates, combined roles, capacity estimates, WIP numbers and review order are team proposals. Older weekly slides inform the sequence of finishing and demonstration, not the current submission deadline. Check the university course system for the definitive deadline. Using Scrum terminology or a specific number of cards does not itself establish an assessment grade.
