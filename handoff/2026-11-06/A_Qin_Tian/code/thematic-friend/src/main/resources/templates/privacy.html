ABOUT MINGLE

Make room for a natural connection.

Mingle is a local web prototype for a six-person thematic project. Use fictional accounts and profiles, without real contact details or sensitive information.

Profiles and messages

Accounts, password hashes, profiles, likes, skips, connections, messages, blocks and reports are stored in the local database chosen by the operator. Only the two participants can read a conversation through the website. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Your choices

You can edit or hide your profile, end connections, block people and report concerns. Hiding a profile does not close existing conversations. Unblocking does not restore a closed connection.

Prototype limits

This prototype is for people aged 18 and over. The checkbox is a self-declaration, not identity verification. Live moderation, account recovery, self-service data deletion and production hosting are not implemented.

To clear demonstration data, the local operator should stop the application and follow the database instructions in the handover guide. Privacy notices, data management and deployment settings need further work before use by real users.
